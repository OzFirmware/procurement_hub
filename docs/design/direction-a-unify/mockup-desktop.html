Payment events (dashed markers) interleave with order events because they are a separate axis. A five-step progress bar cannot show that, and cannot show On Hold at all.

What can happen next

Received Goods arrived and checked. Terminal — solid pills cannot be moved on.
On Hold Pause without losing the stage. Returns to In Transit, or steps back to Ordered.

The system in one screen

Two shapes carry the two axes. A filled pill is a workflow status; a solid fill inside that pill means the state is terminal. An outlined square tag is a payment status. They can never be read as one scale.
