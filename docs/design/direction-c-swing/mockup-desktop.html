Reading the stamp. Three cells, always in this order. Stage is where the request sits in the buying pipeline. State is whether it is running, paused or finished — On hold and Rejected are states, not stages, because either can happen from almost anywhere and both are reversible. Pay runs independently of both: PR-2026-0139 is received and paid, PR-2026-0140 is approved and unpaid. Not recorded is drawn hatched, and means exactly that — the sheet stores one status word, so when a request is put on hold the stage it paused at is genuinely unknown until the audit log is read. It is never guessed.
